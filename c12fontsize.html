Breed History

The Briard, whic is believed to have originated in France, has been bred for centuries to herd and to protect sheep. The breed was used by the french Army as sentries, messengers and to search for wounted soldiers because of its fine sense of hearing. Briards were used in the Firs World war Almostto the point of extinction. Currently the population of Briards is slowly recovering. Charlemagne,Napoleon, Thomas Jefferson and Lafayette all owned briards.
