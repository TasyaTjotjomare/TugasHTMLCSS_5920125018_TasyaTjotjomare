Magnolia

Magnolia grandiflora. commonly known as the Southern magnolia or bull bay,is a tree of the family Magnoliaceae native to the southeastern united States. from coastal Virginia south to central florida, and west to eastern Texas and Oklahoma. Reaching 27,5 m (90 ft) in height, it is a large striking evergreen tree with large dark green leaves and large white fragrand flowers. Widely cultivaled around the world. over a hundred cultivars have been bred and marketed commeercially. The timber is hard and heavy, and has been used commeercially to make furniture.pallets.and veneer
